# Forge Master Subscription Agreement

**Effective Date:** September 2, 2026
**Version:** 1.0

This Forge Master Subscription Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into between Forge Solutions, Corp., a Delaware corporation ("Forge"), and the customer identified in the applicable Order Form ("Customer"). Forge and Customer are each a "Party" and together the "Parties." This Agreement is incorporated by reference into, and governs, each Order Form that references it. Capitalized terms not defined in an Order Form have the meanings given in this Agreement.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 Service. "Service" means the Forge jobsite documentation and estimating platform made available by Forge at forge.equipment and related applications, together with any updates Forge provides during the Term.

1.2 Seat. "Seat" means a single named user authorized to access the Service under Customer's account.

1.3 Customer Data. "Customer Data" means the data, files, photos, jobsite documentation, measurements, scopes, estimates, and other content that Customer or its users submit to or generate in the Service.

1.4 Order Form. "Order Form" means an ordering document that references this Agreement, sets out the plan, Seats, Fees, Effective Date, and Term, and is signed or otherwise accepted by both Parties. Each Order Form incorporates this Agreement by reference.

1.5 Term. "Term" means the Initial Term stated in the applicable Order Form, together with any Renewal Terms.

## 2. Subscription and License Grant

2.1 Access. Subject to this Agreement and payment of the Fees, Forge grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable right to access and use the Service during the Term for Customer's internal business purposes, limited to the number of Seats purchased under the applicable Order Form.

2.2 Restrictions. Customer will not, and will not permit any third party to: (a) resell, sublicense, or make the Service available to any third party except Customer's authorized users; (b) copy, modify, or create derivative works of the Service; (c) reverse engineer or attempt to derive the source code of the Service, except as permitted by law; (d) use the Service to build a competing product; or (e) remove or obscure any proprietary notices.

2.3 Named Users. Each Seat is for one named user and may not be shared. Customer is responsible for all activity under its account and for maintaining the confidentiality of its credentials.

## 3. Seats and Additional Seats

3.1 Included Seats. The applicable Order Form states the plan, the number of included Seats, and the Base Subscription Fee.

3.2 Adding Seats. Customer may add Seats at any time during the Term at the Additional Seat Fee stated in the Order Form. Added Seats are billed on a prorated basis for the remainder of the then-current billing month and at the full monthly rate thereafter, for the remainder of the Term.

3.3 Reducing Seats. Seat reductions take effect at the start of the next Renewal Term. Customer remains responsible for Fees for all purchased Seats through the end of the then-current Term.

## 4. Term and Renewal

4.1 Initial Term. Each Order Form begins on its stated Effective Date and continues for the Initial Term stated in that Order Form.

4.2 Renewal. Each Order Form automatically renews for successive Renewal Terms of the same length as its Initial Term unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 30 days before the end of the then-current Term.

4.3 Price on Renewal. Fees for a Renewal Term will be the same as the prior Term unless Forge gives Customer at least 30 days' written notice of a price change before the Renewal Term begins.

## 5. Fees and Payment

5.1 Fees. Customer will pay the Base Subscription Fee and any Additional Seat Fees stated in the applicable Order Form. All Fees are billed monthly in advance, beginning on the Effective Date, unless the Order Form states otherwise.

5.2 Payment Method. Customer authorizes Forge (or its payment processor) to charge Customer's payment method on file for all Fees when due.

5.3 Committed Term; No Refunds. The Initial Term and each Renewal Term stated in an Order Form are non-cancelable commitments except as expressly stated in Section 15. Fees are non-refundable except where required by law. If Customer stops using the Service before the end of a Term, Customer remains obligated to pay the Fees for the full Term.

5.4 Late Payments. Fees not paid when due accrue interest at 1.5% per month (or the maximum rate permitted by law, if lower) from the due date until paid. Customer will reimburse Forge for reasonable costs of collection.

5.5 Taxes. Fees are exclusive of taxes. Customer is responsible for all sales, use, and similar taxes, excluding taxes on Forge's net income.

6.2 Acceptable Use. Customer will not use the Service to store or transmit unlawful, infringing, or harmful material, or in a way that interferes with the Service or other customers.
